## Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At **6G Network Evolution** (Telcodex.io), accessible from **https://telcodex.io/**, we respect the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and the choices you have. We aim to collect as little personal data as possible.

### 1. Who We Are

This website is an independent information resource about 6G standardization. It is not affiliated with 3GPP or any company. For privacy questions, contact us at contact@telcodex.io.

### 2. Cookies and Consent

We use strictly necessary storage to remember your cookie choices, which is required for the site to function. We do not load any advertising or analytics cookies until you give consent through our cookie banner. You can accept all, reject all non-essential cookies, choose per category, or change your choice at any time using the "Cookie settings" link in the footer. See our Cookie Policy for details.

### 3. Hosting and Server Logs

Our website is hosted by Netlify. Like most hosting providers, Netlify may automatically process technical information such as IP addresses, browser type, and request timestamps to deliver the site securely and reliably. This processing is necessary to operate the website. Please refer to Netlify's own privacy policy for how it handles this data.

### 4. Google AdSense and Advertising

With your consent, we display advertising through Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads, and may process personal data (such as device identifiers and approximate location) to do so. If you do not consent to advertising, these cookies are not loaded. You can also manage personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings (https://www.google.com/settings/ads) and review how Google uses data from its partner sites at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

### 5. Analytics

We do not currently use analytics cookies. If analytics are introduced in future, they will be loaded only with your consent, disclosed in this policy, and added to the consent categories in the cookie banner.

### 6. Third-Party Privacy Policies

This Privacy Policy does not cover the practices of third parties such as Google or other advertisers and linked websites. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies for information on their data handling and opt-out options.

### 7. Your Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and others)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. California residents may opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information using the "Do Not Sell or Share My Info" link in the footer, which disables advertising for your browser. We also honour the Global Privacy Control browser signal for advertising. To exercise any right, contact us at contact@telcodex.io. EU and UK users may also lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority.

### 8. Children

This site is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

### 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the latest revision, and material changes will trigger a renewed cookie-consent request.

### 10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@telcodex.io.
